 Aspect Reviewer - Comprehensive Critical Review

 Use when a plan or decision, or completed work needs critical scrutiny for gaps, overlooked risks, and unexamined assumptions.
 Methodology (The 4 Pillars)
 1. Premises (Given Truth): List all unchallenged, foundational premises (e.g., data stability, fixed budget). These must be explicitly verified before proceeding.
 2. Hidden Risks (Worst-Case): Identify potential failure modes (e.g., dependency failure, resource exhaustion). Calculate impact and probability for major gaps.
 3. Alternative Pathways: Compare the proposed solution's trade-offs (Time vs. Cost vs. Effort vs. Risk) against 2 credible alternatives. Present the structured comparison; never
 recommend a single path.
 4. Edge Cases: Test boundaries (e.g., input = 0; constraint removed). Identify all unresolved stakeholders.
 Final Output
 The final deliverable must conclude: "The plan appears robust, but the single most vulnerable point is [Specific Premise/Risk]." Never provide simple approval.
